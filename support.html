Typical response time: 2–3 business days

Before you email

  • Include your iPhone or Apple Watch model and OS version
  • Describe what you were doing and what you expected to happen
  • Attach a screenshot or short recording when useful
  • Do not send sensitive Health data unless necessary

Common topics

  • Workout tracking, plans, alternatives, and rest timers
  • Apple Health or Apple Watch integration
  • Imports, exports, iCloud backup, and data deletion
  • Premium purchases, billing, and restoring access
  • Bug reports, accessibility issues, and feature feedback

Subscriptions and refunds

QuickLift subscriptions are billed by Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Purchase restoration is also available inside QuickLift.

Refund eligibility and payment disputes are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

QuickLift is local-first. You can manage app data and exports inside QuickLift. Apple Health permissions are controlled in Apple Health or System Settings, and iCloud storage is controlled through your Apple Account.
